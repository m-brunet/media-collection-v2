## Turning leaf (original, generated)

The Turning leaf icon of every set (`turning-leaf.svg`) was generated with GPT Astra for Media Collection, drawn in the
style of the set's pack, and exported from the Doop canvas `shiKEeCX2V`. It is original artwork, not an adaptation of a
specific icon of the pack: checked on 2026-11-09, it shares no path with the set's icons or with the published leaf
icons of Lucide, Iconoir and Game Icons, and its pixel overlap with them is no higher than with unrelated icons. No pack
licence or attribution applies to it; the credits page states that these icons were generated in the style of the sets.
